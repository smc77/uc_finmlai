# Week 3 — Optional Background

The lectures, demos, and summary are self-contained. These readings provide
derivations, historical context, and additional examples; no assessment depends
on completing them.

## Aligned textbook chapters

- **Chapter 7 — Linear Regression for Returns: Baselines and Rank IC**
- **Chapter 9 — Regularization: Ridge, Lasso, and Elastic Net**

### Chapter 7 focus

- OLS as the simplest serious conditional-mean model;
- robust and quantile regression as changes in the objective or target;
- feasible baselines and predictive R²;
- forecast magnitude versus ordering;
- time-series Spearman versus cross-sectional rank IC; and
- the score-to-position ledger, turnover, costs, and reconciliation.

### Chapter 9 focus

- selection as search;
- ill-conditioning and prospective bias–variance tradeoffs;
- ridge, lasso, and elastic-net penalties;
- standardization and the meaning of penalty strength; and
- sparsity as a sample-dependent hypothesis, not discovery.

**Optional exercises:** Chapter 7 exercises 1–5 and Chapter 9 exercises 1–4.

## Free statistical-learning references

- James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani, and Taylor, [*An Introduction to
  Statistical Learning*](https://www.statlearning.com/) — Chapters 3 and 6 give
  a gentler treatment of linear regression, subset selection, ridge, and lasso.
- Hastie, Tibshirani, and Friedman, [*The Elements of Statistical
  Learning*](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/) — Chapter 3 gives a more
  mathematical treatment of linear methods and regularization.
